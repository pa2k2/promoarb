Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

What we collect

  • Payments: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; Stripe shares your email, subscription status, and card last-4 with us solely to verify access.
  • Email alerts: if you sign up for new-offer alerts, your email address is stored with our email provider (Resend) until you unsubscribe.
  • On your device: your access token, tracked-offer progress, custom promos, and state selection live in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device except the token, which is sent to our API to authenticate you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, no sale of personal data.
  • No collection of betting, brokerage, or bank account details — we never ask for credentials to any third-party institution.

Service providers

Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email alerts), Google Fonts (typefaces). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function and is governed by its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Subscription records persist in Stripe while your subscription is active and per Stripe's retention rules afterward. To unsubscribe from alerts or request deletion of your data, email promoarbs@gmail.com. Clearing your browser storage removes all local data.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the date line above.
