07 · The method

How a combo works

Three moves. The order matters — that's the whole trick.

01

Take the protected side

Open the loss-protection book (say BetMGM's $1,500 first-bet offer) and bet one side of a game. If it wins, you're paid in cash. If it loses, the stake comes back as bonus bets.

02

Hedge on the guaranteed side

On a second book with a guaranteed offer (like bet-and-get bonus bets), take the opposite side of the same game. Its bonus pays out regardless of the result.

03

Convert and collect

One side of your hedge always wins in cash, and the bonus bets convert to roughly 70¢ on the dollar by hedging them the same way. Either branch of the slip lands on the same number.

09 · Questions

Fair questions

Is any of this legal?
Yes. Signup bonuses are marketing offers — you're taking companies up on deals they advertise, and meeting the exact terms they wrote. Sportsbook promo arbitrage doesn't break any law, but it does typically violate sportsbook terms of service, which means accounts can be limited or closed (see below). Bank, brokerage, and card bonuses have no such issue.
Will my accounts get limited or banned?
Sportsbooks may limit accounts that only ever bet promos — it's the main occupational hazard, and our playbooks cover pacing to reduce it. Banks can deny a bonus if you don't meet terms precisely, and issuers like Chase have formal rules (5/24) that we track per offer. Brokerage transfer bonuses have holding periods — leave early and they claw back.
Do I owe taxes on bonuses?
Generally yes on bank/brokerage bonuses (they arrive on a 1099-INT/1099-MISC as ordinary income) and on net gambling winnings. Credit-card points earned from spending are generally not taxable. A $400 bank bonus still nets ~$300 after taxes for most filers. This is general information, not tax advice.
How much time does this actually take?
Most cash offers take 15–60 minutes of active work — the board shows an estimated $/hr for every offer, and the sort button ranks by it. Sportsbook combos take about an hour each including the hedge. The bankroll planner sequences everything so you're never wondering what's next.
My state doesn't have legal sports betting. Is this still worth it?
Yes — roughly half the board's value is banks, brokerages, and cards, which work nationwide. Set the state filter and the board, stats, and planner all recalculate to what you can actually do.
Can I cancel?
